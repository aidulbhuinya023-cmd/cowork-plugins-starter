# NDA Triage

## Steps

1. Read `legal-team-context` for NDA thresholds and the approved template.
2. Check: mutual or one-way, definition of confidential information, term and survival, residuals clause, non-solicit or non-compete language, governing law and venue, injunctive relief, and anything unusual.
3. Return one rating:
   - **GREEN**: matches the standard. Sign through the normal process.
   - **YELLOW**: acceptable with specific edits. List them.
   - **RED**: route to counsel. Say why.

Keep the answer to one screen. Include the single most important concern first.
