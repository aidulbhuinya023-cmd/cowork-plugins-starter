# Clause Explainer

## Output

- **What it says**: two sentences, plain words
- **Why it matters**: the practical effect on the company
- **How it commonly looks**: market-standard versus aggressive versions
- **Questions to ask counsel**

Do not state conclusions about enforceability. Note that results vary by jurisdiction and recommend attorney review for any decision.
