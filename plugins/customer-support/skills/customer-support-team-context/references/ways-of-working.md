# Ways of Working: Customer Support

> **Team lead:** edit freely. Write rules as you would tell a new hire. Examples beat adjectives.

## Tools of record

- Ticket system: EDIT ME
- Help center: EDIT ME
- On-call and incident channel: EDIT ME

## Standard workflows

- Triage within 15 minutes of arrival
- Escalate with a brief, never a bare link
- Close the loop with the customer after a fix ships

## Tone and style

- Warm, specific, never defensive
- No jargon the customer did not use first
- Apologize once, then fix

## Approvals and guardrails

- Never share another customer's data.
- Do not promise dates or compensation outside the approval rules.
- Draft replies; a human sends them.
