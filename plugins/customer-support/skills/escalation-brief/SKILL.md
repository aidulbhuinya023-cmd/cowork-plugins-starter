# Escalation Brief

Write the brief so the receiving team can act without asking follow-up questions.

## Template

- **Title**: symptom, not guess at cause
- **Customer and tier**
- **Impact**: who and how many are affected, and revenue or contract risk
- **Timeline**: what happened and when, with ticket links
- **What we already tried**
- **Reproduction steps and environment**
- **Logs, screenshots, and links**
- **The ask**: one specific request
- **Urgency and deadline**
