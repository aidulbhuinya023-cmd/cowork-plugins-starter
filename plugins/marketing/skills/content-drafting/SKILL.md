# Content Drafting

## Steps

1. Confirm audience, goal, and call to action. Infer them from context when possible rather than asking.
2. Read `marketing-team-context` for voice, banned words, and the claims policy.
3. For anything longer than 300 words, share an outline first and wait for a nod.
4. Draft in the brand voice. Offer two headline or subject-line options.
5. If ~~SEO tool is connected, pick one primary keyword and work it in naturally.

## Checks before returning

- Every statistic or customer claim has a source or is flagged for substantiation.
- No competitor disparagement unless the team context allows comparison claims.
- The call to action appears once and is specific.
