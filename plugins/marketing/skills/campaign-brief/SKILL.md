# Campaign Brief

## Steps

1. Read `marketing-team-context` for brand voice, personas, channel list, and the brief template.
2. If the objective, audience, or timeframe is missing, ask for those (at most three questions). Otherwise proceed.
3. Write the brief.

## Output

- **Objective and KPI**: one measurable target
- **Audience**: persona and segment, with the pain we address
- **Key message and proof points**
- **Offer and call to action**
- **Channels and formats**: with the reason for each
- **Timeline**: milestones and owners
- **Assets needed**
- **Measurement plan**: what ~~marketing analytics or ~~CRM will report
- **Risks and approvals**: legal review, brand review, dependencies

Leave budget as a placeholder unless the user provides it.
