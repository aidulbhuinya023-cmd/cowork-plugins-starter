# Marketing plugin

Plan campaigns, draft on-brand content, and report on performance using your CRM, analytics, design, and email marketing tools.

## Slash commands

| Command | What it does |
|---------|--------------|
| `/campaign-brief` | Draft a campaign brief |
| `/draft-content` | Draft on-brand marketing content |
| `/performance-report` | Summarize marketing performance |

## Skills

| Skill | What it covers |
|-------|----------------|
| `campaign-brief` | One-page campaign brief with goal, audience, channels, and measurement |
| `content-drafting` | Drafts blogs, emails, social posts, and landing copy in your brand voice |
| `performance-report` | Channel-by-channel performance summary with hypotheses |
| `marketing-team-context` | **Edit this one.** Your terminology, workflows, tone, and guardrails |

## Connectors

Configured in `.mcp.json`: Slack, HubSpot, Canva, Figma, Amplitude, Notion, Ahrefs, Klaviyo, Supermetrics, Google Drive.
See `CONNECTORS.md` for categories, alternatives, and setup notes.

## Make it yours (5 minutes)

1. Open `skills/marketing-team-context/SKILL.md` and replace each `EDIT ME`.
2. Add your team's terms to `skills/marketing-team-context/references/glossary.md`.
3. Describe how your team works in `skills/marketing-team-context/references/ways-of-working.md`.
4. Delete servers you do not use from `.mcp.json`.
5. Bump `version` in `.claude-plugin/plugin.json` so teammates know it changed.
