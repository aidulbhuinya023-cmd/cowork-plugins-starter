# Follow-Up Drafting

Turn a meeting into a follow-up email and a CRM note.

## Steps

1. Get the source: a transcript or summary from ~~conversation intelligence, or text the user pasted.
2. Read `sales-team-context` for tone, length, and email conventions.
3. Draft the email:
   - Three bullets on what the customer said they care about, in their words
   - Commitments from both sides, with dates
   - One clear next step with a proposed time
4. Draft a CRM note: summary, next step, and a recommended stage change if the evidence supports one.

Draft only. Never send email or edit ~~CRM records without explicit confirmation.
