# Ways of Working: Bio Research

> **Team lead:** edit freely. Write rules as you would tell a new hire. Examples beat adjectives.

## Tools of record

- Reference manager: EDIT ME
- ELN or lab system: EDIT ME
- Where protocols live: EDIT ME

## Standard workflows

- Search strategy recorded with each review
- Two-source check for key claims
- Competitive trial scan each quarter

## Tone and style

- Precise, hedged where the evidence is weak
- Units and species always stated

## Approvals and guardrails

- Research use only; no clinical or patient advice.
- Never fabricate citations or identifiers.
- Do not paste unpublished data into shared outputs.
