# Product Management plugin

Write PRDs, prioritize roadmap candidates, and synthesize customer feedback using your tracker, design files, analytics, and docs.

## Slash commands

| Command | What it does |
|---------|--------------|
| `/write-prd` | Draft a PRD from an idea or problem |
| `/prioritize` | Score and sequence roadmap candidates |
| `/synthesize-feedback` | Find themes in feedback or interviews |

## Skills

| Skill | What it covers |
|-------|----------------|
| `prd-writing` | Drafts a PRD with evidence, metrics, and open questions |
| `roadmap-prioritization` | Scores and sequences candidates with transparent assumptions |
| `feedback-synthesis` | Turns scattered feedback into ranked, evidence-backed themes |
| `product-management-team-context` | **Edit this one.** Your terminology, workflows, tone, and guardrails |

## Connectors

Configured in `.mcp.json`: Slack, Linear, Atlassian (Jira, Confluence), Asana, Notion, Figma, Amplitude, Pendo, Intercom, Fireflies.
See `CONNECTORS.md` for categories, alternatives, and setup notes.

## Make it yours (5 minutes)

1. Open `skills/product-management-team-context/SKILL.md` and replace each `EDIT ME`.
2. Add your team's terms to `skills/product-management-team-context/references/glossary.md`.
3. Describe how your team works in `skills/product-management-team-context/references/ways-of-working.md`.
4. Delete servers you do not use from `.mcp.json`.
5. Bump `version` in `.claude-plugin/plugin.json` so teammates know it changed.
